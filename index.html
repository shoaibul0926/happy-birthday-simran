Kuch rishte alfaazon se nahi, ehsaason se pehchaane jaate hain, Kuch log zindagi mein aakar apne ban jaate hain... ❤️ Ek saal se bhi zyada ka safar, aur rishta aaj bhi wahi hai, Na apnapan kam hua, na baaton mein koi kami hai... Waqt guzarta gaya, magar woh pehli si baat aaj bhi barkaraar hai, Kuch toh khaas hai is rishte mein, jo har guzarte din ke saath aur shandaar hai... Na jaane kaise itne kam waqt mein itni gehri pehchaan ban gayi, Kabhi hasi, kabhi baatein, kabhi yaadein, ek khoobsurat kahaani ban gayi... Ek saal se zyada beet gaya, magar lagta hai jaise kal ki baat hai, Shayad kuch rishte waqt se nahi, dil ke apne jazbaat se bante hain... ❤️ Teri fikr karne ka andaaz, tumhara har baar samjhaana, Meri khamoshi ko padhna aur bina kahe haal jaan jaana... Teri har chhoti si concern mein ek alag hi pyaar nazar aata hai, Sach kahun toh tere hone se ek bade behen ka ehsaas aata hai... Jab zindagi ke raaste mushkil hon, jab har taraf andhera ho, Jab dil mein hazaar sawaal hon aur samajh na aaye savera ho... Tab tumhara saath mujhe ek naya hausla de jaata hai, Tera ek chhota sa message bhi chehre par muskurahat le aata hai... Meri kamyaabi mein khush hona, meri nakaami mein samjhaana, Mere bure waqt mein saath rehna, aur achhe waqt mein hausla badhaana... Meri lowest phase mein bhi tune apnapan kabhi kam nahi hone diya, Aur meri highest moments mein bhi tune mujhe kabhi akela mehsoos nahi hone diya... Har kisi ko naseeb nahi hoti aisi badi behen ki chhaon, Jiske hone se aasaan lage zindagi ki har mushkil raah... Main shayad har baar apni feelings ko bayaan nahi kar paata, Magar tumhara ye Chotu Bhai tere pyaar aur fikr ko kabhi bhula nahi paata... 🥹❤️ Aur ab baat karte hain hamari TELEPATHY ki! 😂 Kabhi main sochun kuch aur, tere lafzon mein wahi baat aa jaaye, Kabhi main kuch kehne hi wala hoon, aur tumhara message pehle aa jaaye... Na koi signal, na koi notification, na koi pehle se taiyaari hai, Phir bhi ek doosre ke dimaag mein chal kya raha hai, uski poori khabardari hai... Kabhi ek hi waqt par ek hi khayal ka aa jaana, Kabhi bina pooche ek doosre ki baat samajh jaana... Ye kaisi telepathy hai, iska jawaab aaj tak mila nahi, Lagta hai hamare dimaag ka connection abhi tak kisi ne samjha hi nahi! 😂 Duniya ke liye ye sirf ek coincidence hoga, Magar hamare liye ye ek alag hi connection hoga... Na jaane kitni baar ye kamaal dikhaya hai, Bina kuch kahe bhi ek doosre ko samajh paaya hai... ❤️ Aur hamari SAME THINKING ka toh alag hi afsaana hai, Kabhi meri soch teri, toh kabhi tumhara khayal mera puraana hai... Opinions ho ya reactions, har jagah ek jaisa nazariya mil jaata hai, Kabhi-kabhi toh lagta hai, hum dono ka software ek hi version se aata hai! 😂 Har baat par ek jaisi soch, har situation par milta-julta jawaab, Kabhi bina samjhaaye samajh jaana, kabhi bina pooche mil jaana jawaab... Aise log zindagi mein baar-baar nahi mila karte, Jo dil ki baat samajh lein, woh har kisi ke naseeb mein nahi hua karte... Na har rishte ko naam dena zaroori hota hai, Na har apnepan ko lafzon mein bayaan karna zaroori hota hai... Kuch log bas dil mein apni jagah bana lete hain, Aur phir zindagi bhar ke liye khaas ban jaate hain... ❤️ Aur sabse zaroori, Allah se meri dua... 🤲🏻 Jis Rab ne milaya hai, usi se fariyaad karta hoon, Teri khushiyon ke liye har roz dua karta hoon... Tere naseeb mein sirf khushiyan aur sukoon likhe, Tere har kadam par kamyaabi aur barkat likhe... Har buri nazar se teri hifazat farmaye, Har mushkil ghadi mein tujhe aasaani ata farmaye... Tere ghar mein hamesha mohabbat aur barkat rahe, Tere apnon ke chehron par hamesha muskurahat rahe... Jo khwahishein tune khamoshi se apne dil mein sajaayi hain, Allah un sabko tere haq mein behtareen tareeke se poora kare... Duniya ki har khushi tere muqaddar mein shaamil ho, Aur aakhirat mein bhi tujhe behtareen inaam haasil ho... Teri zindagi ka har naya savera khoobsurat ho, Har aane wala kal aaj se zyada behtar ho...
